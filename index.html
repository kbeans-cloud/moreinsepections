How the system works

Four steps, from a homeowner's first click to a booked inspection on your calendar.

  1. 1

    Attract

    We put your business in front of homeowners with Meta ads, a strong offer, and a reason to trust you and act now. We test creative, keep what wins, and cut what doesn't.

  2. 2

    Capture

    Every lead lands in one place, gets qualified, and is tracked from the first click. You always know what each lead cost and where it came from.

  3. 3

    Speed to Lead

    Text automation reaches out within minutes, and a real person calls the lead. No more leads going cold while you're on a roof.

  4. 4

    Book

    Inspections go straight onto your calendar with reminders to cut no-shows. You see the whole pipeline from lead to estimate sent to job booked.

We don't stop at the booked inspection

The inspection goes on your calendar. The day before, our team calls the homeowner to confirm they'll be there, and reminders go out too, so all you have to do is show up to an inspection with the homeowner home. After it, we follow up on your estimates and help you get paid.
